## Why this is worth an hour

The channel currently reaches nobody, so uploading more videos to it changes nothing. Retitling makes the ten
that already exist findable by drivers searching the questions they actually have. The bigger opening is
**commenting on other people's driver videos** — driver YouTube is far larger than driver Facebook, and unlike
Facebook groups, comments are open to any channel. That needs the ProfitPin channel signed in.
