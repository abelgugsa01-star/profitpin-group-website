## SaleLedger — getsaleledger.com

- **Tagline (60 chars):** Monthly auction trust account reconciliation, on your PC
- **Category:** Accounting / Bank Reconciliation / Auction Software
- **Short description (150 chars):** Reconciles an auction house's trust account each month from the settlement export, QuickBooks file and bank statement. Names every exception.
- **Long description:**
  SaleLedger is Windows software for auction houses and estate-sale companies that hold consignor money in a trust account. Each month you bring in three files you already have — the Auction Flex settlement export, the QuickBooks/IIF payout export and the trust account bank statement — and SaleLedger runs the three-way check: bank adjusted balance, trust control balance and consignor subledger total. Every gap becomes a named exception (disbursement mismatch, unmatched deposit, unauthorized transfer to operating, bank fee taken from trust, stale funds) for you to decide. The report ends with the check register state boards ask for, and every period is kept in a local history. Nothing is uploaded; your consignor and bank data stay on your computer. $129/month, first 30 days free, cancel any time.
- **Key features:** Three-way trust reconciliation · Auction Flex, QuickBooks/IIF, CSV/Excel/OFX bank statements · Named exceptions, never guessed · Check register · Local-only processing · Reproducible reports (print, CSV, email)
- **Pricing:** $129.00/month, 30-day free trial, month to month
- **Platform:** Windows 10/11 desktop
- **Support:** support@getsaleledger.com

## LeafLedger — getleafledger.com

- **Tagline:** TTB 5210.5 / 5220.6 and FDA 3852 drafts, reconciled first
- **Category:** Compliance / Regulatory Reporting / Manufacturing
- **Short description:** Reconciles a tobacco manufacturer's or importer's monthly records and prepares TTB 5210.5, 5220.6 and FDA 3852 report drafts with the evidence attached.
- **Long description:**
  LeafLedger is Windows software for domestic tobacco manufacturers and importers who file monthly with TTB and FDA. Map your production, transfer, shipping and inventory exports once; each month bring in the files and LeafLedger rolls every product class forward in exact arithmetic (opening + additions − removals = closing), compares book to physical count, ties tax-paid removals to the return, and flags every missing document, unit inconsistency or cut-off problem by name. It then prepares TTB 5210.5 / 5220.6 and FDA 3852 draft packages with a reproducible evidence trail and immutable history. It never guesses classifications, tax amounts or conversion factors, and it never certifies or files — your officer does. Records stay on your computer. $199/month, first 30 days free.
- **Key features:** Class-by-class roll-forward · Book-to-count comparison · Tax tie-out · Missing-document flags · TTB 5210.5, 5220.6, FDA 3852 drafts · Evidence packages and history · Local-only
- **Pricing:** $199.00/month, 30-day free trial
- **Platform:** Windows 10/11 desktop
- **Support:** support@getleafledger.com
- **Link:** https://getleafledger.com/?utm_source=directory&utm_campaign=capterra

## SubLimit — getsublimit.com

- **Tagline:** Limitations on subcontracting, calculated from your own records
- **Category:** Government Contracting / Compliance / Workforce Reporting
- **Short description:** Exact FAR 52.219-14 / 13 CFR 125.6 subcontracting-limit calculations from payroll, subcontractor and government-receipt exports, plus workforce report drafts.
- **Long description:**
  SubLimit is Windows software for small-business federal contractors on set-aside contracts. Configure the contract once (type, limit, period); each reporting period import your payroll/timekeeping, subcontractor and government-receipt CSV/XLSX files, record verified similarly-situated statuses with evidence, and get the exact counted-subcontracting percentage against the 50% / 85% / 75% limit — with every contribution linked to its source row and every unknown listed as an exception instead of a guess. Prepare the supported OCC monthly workforce report as an unsigned Word draft from the same records. Password-protected local workspace, encrypted backups, full history. No uploads, no telemetry; your officer reviews, certifies and submits. $149/month, first 30 days free, one company on two computers.
- **Key features:** Payment-based subcontracting-limit calculation · Similarly situated tracking with evidence · Source-row traceability · OCC workforce report draft (DOCX) · Protected workspace and encrypted backups · Local-only

- **Category:** Field Service / Inventory / HVAC-Plumbing-Electrical
- **Short description:** Reads supplier invoices against your ServiceTitan pricebook export, matches existing items, flags new parts, units and price changes, and produces clean updates to import back.
- **Long description:**
  StockStart keeps a contractor's ServiceTitan pricebook current as supplier invoices arrive. Export your pricebook once; then drop in each week's invoices, receipts, photos and spreadsheets.
